Support for Hi Memo.

Use these notes for common local-data, backup, reminder, and Google Drive questions. For a problem report, include your device, operating system, app version, and what you were trying to do.

Contact

For feedback, bug reports, support questions, or privacy requests, email nguyenanhtiep12@gmail.com.

You can edit or cancel the email draft before sending it from Hi Memo.

Google Drive sync

Google Drive sync is optional. If access was revoked or the account changed, reconnect the same intended account from the Sync sheet.

Disconnecting does not delete remote app data. Use the separate confirmed removal action if you want Hi Memo to remove its current Google Drive sync files.

Local data and backups

Before restoring a backup or clearing app data, create and verify a current backup if you need to preserve existing notes.

Copies exported or shared outside Hi Memo remain at the destination you selected until you delete them there.

Privacy choices
