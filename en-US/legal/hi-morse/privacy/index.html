Hi Morse is a Morse code learning, practice and translation app developed by BaTip. This policy explains the information the app handles and how it is used. Contact: nguyenanhtiep12@gmail.com.

Summary

You do not need a Hi Morse account to learn and practice. Morse content, learning progress, custom sets, import drafts and app preferences are stored locally. Pro purchases and restoration use your Apple App Store or Google Play account; the app does not create a BaTip account linking the two stores.

On Android and iOS, Usage analytics is enabled by default to help improve the app. You can change it in More → Privacy → Usage analytics without affecting learning or Pro access. Crash reporting is separate and is not disabled by the Usage analytics switch.

Information the app does not request

Hi Morse does not require your name, email address or phone number to learn and practice, access your contacts, or collect precise location, photos, audio recordings or camera images. If you choose to email support, BaTip receives your sender address and the content you send. The store handles payment information; the app does not receive your card number or bank details.

Hi Morse does not automatically upload Morse content or custom sets to BaTip servers. App-authored Analytics events do not include Morse text, answers, practice targets, custom-set names or contents, clipboard contents, feedback email contents, audio recordings, search queries or manual keying rhythms. Your choice to share files or email content is described separately below.

Information stored on your device

Hi Morse may store locally:

  • Language, navigation, audio, Morse keyboard and other app preferences.
  • Recent patterns, progress, streaks, answer history and learning-session state.
  • Custom-set names, content and internal identifiers, and your Free-set selection.
  • Draft content you paste or import, including before you confirm a library import.
  • Your Usage analytics preference and feedback-invitation timing state.
  • Pro access information and timing records used to check offline access.

BaTip does not operate a cloud sync or backup service for learning progress and custom sets. The operating system may back up or transfer some app data depending on your platform and settings. Restoring Pro from the store does not restore learning data. Offline access information is protected separately and is not designed to transfer to another device through a backup.

Pro subscriptions and stores

Apple App Store or Google Play handles Pro purchases, renewals, management and restoration. The app uses store-provided information, such as transaction identifiers or purchase tokens, product identifiers, verification and subscription status, and expiration information where available, to check access. Transaction-processing information (including transactions needing completion), Pro rights and timing records are kept in protected storage on your device for restoration and limited offline use. Pro expiration does not itself delete learning data or custom sets.

BaTip does not operate a private purchase-verification server for this version. Payment and restoration requests still communicate with store services. Apple and Google process accounts, transactions and records under their policies; BaTip may access sales, order or refund reports supplied to developers by the stores. These reports are separate from learning data on your device.

On iOS, the app may contact Apple to check Internet access when checking Pro. Apple receives connection information such as your IP address; this request does not include learning content or Pro transaction data.

Importing and sharing practice sets

When you select a file, open a shared file with Hi Morse or paste content, the app reads the file name and contents or the pasted text to validate and preview it. Content may remain in a draft so you can continue later. The app does not modify or delete the source file you selected.

Sharing a custom set creates a file copy containing the set name, practice content and format information. It does not contain Pro rights, purchase credentials or learning history. The receiving person or app can read this file. You choose the destination through the operating system's share sheet; that service may upload the file or send it to others according to your choice and its own policy.

Import or share copies may remain in local or temporary app storage. Old share files are cleaned up during later sharing or operating-system cache cleanup; they are not guaranteed to be deleted when the share sheet closes. Deleting a set in Hi Morse or letting Pro expire does not recall copies you have sent or saved elsewhere. Manage those copies at their destination or with the recipient.

Usage analytics

Hi Morse uses Firebase Analytics on Android and iOS. Usage analytics is enabled by default without a separate data-sharing confirmation dialog. You can change it in More → Privacy → Usage analytics.

When enabled, the app may send limited events about:

  • Screens, dialogs or learning areas viewed.
  • Fixed interface components and actions, such as opening a setting, starting output, copying or moving to another exercise.
  • Practice and Course session starts/completions, counts, broad accuracy and duration ranges, skills, levels and content-source categories.
  • Answer outcomes using fixed categories: skill, level, input method, correct/wrong result, code set and a broad position bucket.
  • Starting sound, flashlight or vibration output, and an allowlist of commands and preferences.

Firebase may also collect automatic installation, session and app-activity events. On Android, the Firebase/Google Play integration may record purchase or subscription events, including product identifiers, value, currency and quantity. These events do not contain card numbers or bank details.

Analytics may include app version, broad device and operating-system information, a Firebase-generated app installation identifier, selected language and a country/region Google estimates from network information. Granular GA4 location and device collection is disabled. The app does not request precise-location permission for Analytics, set an account or custom user ID, or grant advertising storage/personalization consent in its Analytics integration. This data is used to understand and improve Hi Morse.

Turning Usage analytics off stops new Analytics collection from the app and resets Analytics data stored locally by Firebase. It does not delete progress, preferences, Pro access or data already sent to Google. Turning it on resumes collection for future activity; the app does not backfill activity from when it was off or send an event recording the switch choice. This switch does not cancel a subscription or delete the store's separate transaction records.

Clipboard and flashlight

Hi Morse accesses the clipboard when you use actions such as Copy or Paste to perform that action. Content pasted for set import may be saved in a draft as described above.

When you choose flashlight playback or feedback, the app switches the flashlight on and off to represent Morse code. It does not capture photos, videos or camera images.

Crash reporting

Hi Morse uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify and fix errors. Diagnostic information may include crash reports, exceptions, stack traces, app and operating-system versions, device model/state and crash-reporting identifiers. The bundled SDKs may also send session information and network connection type to measure app stability, including when no crash occurs.

This data is used for stability, not advertising. When Usage analytics is enabled, Analytics events may be attached as diagnostic context. The Usage analytics switch does not disable Crashlytics.

See Firebase privacy, Firebase data disclosure guidance and Google's Privacy Policy.

Support email

When you choose feedback, bug reporting or contact actions, your email app may prepare a message containing app version, operating system, language, current screen and time. You can edit or remove these details before sending it yourself. BaTip receives your sender address and the content you send to provide support and improve the app.

Third-party services

  • Firebase Analytics for usage measurement and supported automatic events.
  • Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting.
  • Apple App Store or Google Play for Pro transactions, restoration, subscription management, app ratings and store-listing actions.
  • File providers, sharing destinations, email apps or browsers you choose.
  • Operating-system backup or device-transfer services you use.

These services handle information under their own policies. Subscription information is in the Hi Morse Terms.

Children

Hi Morse is a general-audience educational utility, not directed specifically to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a support email, contact us so we can address its removal.

Retention and deletion

Learning data and custom sets generally remain until you delete them in the app, clear app data or uninstall. Operating-system backups and shared files are managed separately; uninstalling Hi Morse does not delete external copies. Some operating-system security items may survive uninstall; the app discards an old Pro cache when it detects that the cache does not belong to the current installation. The offline access limit is not an automatic deletion period for stored transaction or Pro access information.

Crashlytics retains reports according to Firebase's retention practices. Emails are retained as long as needed to resolve requests and improve the app. Firebase Analytics is configured to retain user/event data for 14 months; new activity does not reset that period. Disabling Analytics does not immediately delete data Google has already received and retained. Stores retain transaction records under their own policies and obligations.

Security

Hi Morse uses platform and service-provider safeguards. Pro access cache is protected using operating-system security mechanisms; this does not mean all learning data or exported files are separately encrypted by the app. Diagnostic data sent to Firebase uses secure connections. No transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Translations, changes and contact

If translations of this policy are inconsistent, the English version controls. This policy and store privacy disclosures will be updated when data handling changes. Contact BaTip at nguyenanhtiep12@gmail.com about privacy or requests concerning data you have sent us.
