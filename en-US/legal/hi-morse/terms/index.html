Hi Morse is a Morse code learning and practice app provided by BaTip. You can use its Free features or subscribe to Hi Morse Pro for the benefits described in the app. Support email: nguyenanhtiep12@gmail.com.

Hi Morse Pro and payments

Hi Morse Pro currently offers monthly and yearly billing with the same benefits. The yearly plan is paid for the full year. The store shows the price, currency, billing period and applicable tax information before you confirm. Apple App Store or Google Play processes the transaction through your store account.

Your subscription renews automatically for the selected period until you cancel renewal. Use Pro → Manage subscription in Hi Morse or manage it directly through the store where you purchased it. Deleting the app does not replace subscription cancellation. Canceling renewal generally retains access until the end of the paid period; refunds, revocations or payment-status changes may affect access according to the store's decision.

Refund requests are handled under the store's policy and your rights under applicable law. For Apple purchases, see requesting a refund. For Google Play, see refund policies or contact BaTip support. Canceling renewal does not itself request a refund.

Access and restoration

Pro currently includes six Pro practice packs, unlimited custom-set count at the app level, bulk import, sharing custom sets and choosing any Course lesson. Content validation, file-size limits and device-storage limits still apply. Free users can still learn the full Course sequentially and use Free features.

To restore Pro, use the account from the store where you purchased it, connect to the internet and choose Restore Pro on a supported device. You can restore purchased access on supported devices using the same store account. Hi Morse does not provide an account linking purchases between Apple App Store and Google Play. Restoring a purchase does not restore learning data or custom sets deleted from a device.

Use Pro offline for up to 12 days after a successful online check. You may need to reconnect sooner to verify your subscription. If the store provides your subscription’s expiry time, offline access ends no later than that time. Otherwise, the app updates your subscription status when you reconnect. When another check is required, reconnect and choose Restore Pro. Opening the app offline does not restart the 12-day window. Refunds and revocations take effect when the app receives the corresponding store status.

Expiration and your data

When Pro ends, access returns to the Free level. Expiration does not itself delete progress or custom sets on the device. The six Pro packs and custom sets above the Free limit remain visible but require Pro for Pro access; up to two sets selected for Free use remain usable. Course returns to sequential requirements and retains lessons actually completed.

Hi Morse stores progress and custom sets locally. BaTip does not operate a cloud sync or backup service for learning data; the operating system may back up or transfer data depending on your platform and settings. Clearing app data, uninstalling or losing the device may lose local data. Shared set files are independent copies and are not recalled when the sender's Pro expires. You are responsible for the rights to content you import or share. Do not distribute unlawful content or content that infringes someone else's rights.
