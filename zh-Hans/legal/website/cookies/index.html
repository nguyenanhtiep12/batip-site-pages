About cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small text values stored by your browser. BaTip also uses local browser storage for preferences. The same consent rules apply to optional analytics storage regardless of the storage technology used.

The Hi Morse and Hi Eye web apps use local browser storage for app settings and progress. Those app values remain on your device and are not sent to Google Analytics by this website.

Essential storage

  • batip.cookieConsent.v1: records your cookie purposes, policy version, and expiry for 6 months
  • batip.locale: remembers a language you explicitly select for 12 months

Essential storage is not used to measure usage or support marketing. Rejecting optional cookies does not prevent the website or web apps from providing their main functions.

Optional Google Analytics cookies

After you consent, Google Analytics may set first-party cookies whose names begin with btp_ga, btp_perf_ga, or btp_mkt_ga. They contain a pseudonymous browser identifier and expire after 6 months from creation. Their expiry is not extended on each visit.

Performance analytics uses sanitized page paths, navigation-link clicks, and limited interface-control clicks identified by fixed action codes. Marketing measurement may use UTM campaign parameters, sanitized referrer paths, and app open or download clicks. BaTip does not send link text or user-entered content with these events and disables Google signals, advertising personalization signals, user-provided data, and user IDs.

Provider and information processed

Google Analytics is provided by Google. A browser request to Google necessarily includes network and device information such as an IP address and user agent. Google Analytics may process the page views and clicks described above, along with general information about the visit, such as the browser, device, approximate country or region, and how you found batip.app.

Duration

Your Google Analytics choice is stored in your browser for 6 months.

Google Analytics cookies expire 6 months after they are created and are not renewed on each visit.

Detailed data in Google Analytics is retained for 14 months. This period does not apply to aggregated statistical reports that Google Analytics may retain separately.

Manage your choices

You can open Cookie settings in the website footer at any time to allow or reject Google Analytics.

If you reject after previously consenting, the website will stop sending new data to Google Analytics and remove the corresponding Google Analytics cookies from this browser.

You can also clear cookies and website data through your browser. The website may then ask for your choice again.

Changes and contact

BaTip will request a new choice when analytics purposes, providers, or this consent policy materially change.

Questions or requests about website data can be sent to nguyenanhtiep12@gmail.com.
