Before contacting support

  • Update to the latest version available on Google Play or the App Store.
  • Restart the app if sound, flash, or playback seems stuck.
  • Check device volume if audio playback is quiet.
  • Check flashlight permission and device support if flash feedback is unavailable.
  • Try a short Morse sample first when testing audio or flashlight playback.

Contact

For feedback, bug reports, or support questions, contact the developer by email at nguyenanhtiep12@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, include your device model, operating system version, app version, selected language, and what you were trying to do.

FAQ

Does Hi Morse need an account?

No. You can use Hi Morse without creating an account.

Does Hi Morse upload the Morse text I type?

No. Morse text and practice input are processed on your device.

Why does the app use the flashlight?

The flashlight is used only when you choose flash feedback or flash playback for Morse code.

Why does feedback email include device details?

Device and app details help diagnose issues. You can edit or remove that information before sending the email.
